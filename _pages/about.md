I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Social and Organisational Psychology at the University of Bremen. My research lies at the intersection of moral and developmental psychology. I investigate what children, adolescents, and adults consider morally right and wrong, who they believe is worthy of moral concern, and what underlies these views.
