I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Social and Organisational Psychology at the University of Bremen.

My research sits at the intersection of moral and developmental psychology. I explore how moral judgements and reasoning evolve across the lifespan - investigating what children, adolescents, and adults consider morally right and wrong, who they believe is worthy of moral concern, and what underlies these beliefs. 

If you are interested in collaborating — particularly on data collection across different countries or within school settings — please feel free to reach out.
